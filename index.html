What we do

Data analytics

Most organisations already hold the data they need. The problem is that it sits in different systems, in different formats, and nobody has time to pull it together. We clean and link it, build the analysis and set up reporting your team can run without us.

  • Dashboards and routine performance reports
  • Statistical modelling and forecasting
  • Machine learning and text analysis
  • Data pipelines and quality checks
  • Training for staff who work with data

Management consulting

We advise leadership teams on strategy, structure and delivery. That includes reviewing how an organisation works today, preparing business cases for new investments, and supporting the planning and management of reforms.

  • Strategic and business planning
  • Organisational and process reviews
  • Policy and regulatory analysis
  • Business cases and feasibility studies
  • Project and programme management support

Impact analysis

We assess whether policies, programmes and investments achieve what they set out to do, and at what cost. We also estimate likely effects before a decision is made, so that households, businesses and the budget are considered up front.

  • Programme and policy evaluation
  • Ex-ante impact and fiscal cost estimates
  • Cost-benefit and value-for-money analysis
  • Social return on investment (SROI)
  • Performance measurement frameworks

Public opinion research

We design and run surveys and qualitative studies to find out what citizens, customers and stakeholders think. We pay close attention to sampling and question wording, because that is where most surveys go wrong.

  • Household, telephone and online surveys
  • Opinion polling and perception tracking
  • Customer and citizen satisfaction studies
  • Focus groups and key informant interviews
